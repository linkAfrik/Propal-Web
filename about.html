How we work

Our four non-negotiable rules.

01

Confidential by default.

No opportunity or counterparty is disclosed before the parties sign confidentiality and non-circumvention terms.

02

Tied to your results.

Our compensation is agreed upfront: success-based on deals, fixed fee for business development support.

03

Selective.

We focus on opportunities we believe are worth pursuing and partners capable of contributing to them.

Transparent.
