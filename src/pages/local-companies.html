Frequently asked questions

Your questions, answered.

How can a local company win a contract it cannot fully deliver alone?

By bringing in a partner for the missing part: equipment, technical capability, references or financing. You keep the client relationship and your role; the partner covers the gap. We identify what is missing and find the right partner.

Will COPHIR take over our client relationship?

No. You remain the company holding the opportunity. Nothing is shared with another party without your agreement, and confidentiality and non-circumvention terms are signed before any disclosure.

What kind of opportunities do you work on?

Tenders, RFQs and direct client needs in West African Oil & Gas: subsea, topside, offshore and marine services, downstream, environmental services and industrial procurement.

How is COPHIR paid?

Compensation is agreed upfront: success-based on deals, or a fixed fee for business development support.

What should we send you to get started?

A short description of the opportunity, the client, the deadline and what you are missing. That is enough for us to tell you whether we can help.
