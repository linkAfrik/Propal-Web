Your questions, answered.

How do you supply equipment to oil and gas projects in West Africa?

Buyers look for compliant products, full documentation (certificates, datasheets), competitive prices and reliable lead times, often with local delivery and after-sales service. Manufacturers without a local presence usually work with a local company that handles import, customs, stock and support.

What does COPHIR do in industrial procurement?

We identify the buyer's need, connect it with qualified manufacturers or suppliers, and with the local partner able to deliver and support the equipment on site.
