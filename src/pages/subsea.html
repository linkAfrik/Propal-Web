In practice

Your questions, answered.

How do you find a local subsea partner in Côte d'Ivoire?

Start from the scope, not from a list of companies. A diving or ROV campaign needs a local company able to handle base logistics, personnel, permits and the local content share of the contract, while technical execution stays with a certified contractor. We qualify local companies on what they actually deliver (people, equipment, past work) before any introduction.

What do operators look for in a subsea bid?

A strong safety record, certified divers and ROV pilots, available equipment, procedures approved by the operator and references on comparable work. A bid that pairs a capable international contractor with a credible local partner answers both the technical and the local content criteria.
